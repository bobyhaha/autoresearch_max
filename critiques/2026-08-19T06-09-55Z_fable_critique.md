### 3. THE RESEARCH SYSTEM

- **The decision-cutoff freeze is a net-negative safeguard as implemented, and I will say so plainly.** Its exemption for controls, combined with per-entry (not per-wave) filtering, converts every freeze into automatic control-only GPU spend: the zloss waves are the proof, twice, at ~4 charged runs of cost and zero scientific yield — directly violating the operator's own post-critique pledge of "no further control-only waves." Entries are already built and validated at queue time by `queue_from_round.py`; freezing their *launch* when a 90-minute council artifact goes stale adds no safety, only idle GPUs and orphaned controls. Fix: (a) gate at wave granularity — if any member is frozen, hold the whole wave; (b) lengthen `max_age_s` from 90 min to a wave-turnaround time, or make staleness block only new queue appends.
- **The 79% control share was partly legitimate (the device model was genuinely needed and it rescued precond from a false null) but at least ten of the W-wave controls and both VE_A/VE_B singletons re-measured known numbers.** Structural fix, not exhortation: change the default wave template from [T,C,C,T]+[C,T,T,C] (50% control) to 3 treatments + 1 rotating control per quad (25% control) now that per-GPU baselines are established; keep the rotating control as a drift sentinel.
- **Registration-time diagnostic check.** 14 of 20 hypotheses declare activation diagnostics nothing emits (critic-r2's grep; I spot-confirmed several). Make `claims.py hyp` refuse any hypothesis whose diagnostic is absent from an emitted-telemetry manifest generated from `make_variant.py`. This converts "INCONCLUSIVE by construction" from a recurring lesson into an impossibility.
- **The monoculture has a literature-side engine.** The claims corpus is itself skewed (optimizer_numeric 322 claims vs attention 32, ve_placement 32, input_pipeline 13), and agenda.py's score rewards usable claims, so the active direction self-reinforces. The hard cap will fire (optimizer_numeric sits at ~11 of 12 counted runs), but the next `lit.py fetch` should be quota'd toward the empty families or the same lock-in recurs one rotation later.
