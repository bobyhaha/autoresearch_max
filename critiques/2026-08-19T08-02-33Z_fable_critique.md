### 4. The research system

Count the ledger: of 41 lessons, roughly **17 are self-inflicted instrument or harness failures** (wave splits, double-booking, three successive wrong slot-offset models before L020, stale host policy L012, queue-sync drift L009, a non-engaging mechanism that burned three runs L014, a diagnostic that measured the lm_head L027, an activation predicate the control also passed L030, `gpu_slack_frac` invalid within the hour L034, device-set substitution L036) against **2 confirmed wins and 3 valid negatives** in ~84 runs. Yes — the instrument-bug rate, not GPU capacity and not idea quality, is currently the binding constraint: nearly every confirmed effect needed a repaired second attempt, so the effective cost per verdict is roughly double the nominal. The mitigating grace is that the *detection* machinery works — verdict.py refuses rather than lies, and every failure became a typed lesson. The fix is to move detection from post-hoc to preflight, three concrete edits:

1. **Diagnostic contract test in `make_variant`/`queue_from_round`**: before any GPU launch, run treatment and control variants for ~10 steps (tiny budget, CPU-timed) and assert the declared activation diagnostic (i) is emitted and (ii) *differs between arms*. This single check would have caught L014, L016, L027, and L030 for free.
2. **Automate L012**: dispatcher refuses to launch a wave unless the host's `direction.py`/`train.py` md5s match the local manifest — a hash check at claim time, not a standing rule in prose.
3. **Pin wave device sets** (§3 block item) and make a split wave *hold* all members (L023) with the stranded-treatment accounting of L031 impossible by construction.

On single-seed: keep it for screening (it is what makes 8-minute quads cheap), but **require one second-seed quad as a gate on platform adoption only** — 8 runs, once per adoption event, is the cheapest insurance against enshrining a seed-42 artifact in every future control. This is a narrow, priced disagreement with the operator instruction, spent exactly where its cost is lowest and its value highest.

### 5. Ranking (expected bpb per GPU-hour; one run ≈ 0.135 GPU-h, a quad pair ≈ 1.1 GPU-h)

3. **swdiv 4→8 dose quad** (8 runs): plausible further −0.001 on a monotone-so-far axis.

**Cheap and possibly nothing:** 5. zloss requeue (8 runs, unknown sign, explore credit). 6. Pending `R3T` tbs=20 and `R3Q` qk_suppress — let them drain; predictions already registered.

**Expensive and possibly large (after the stack verdict):** 7. Second-seed adoption gate (8 runs; buys validity, not bpb). 8.
