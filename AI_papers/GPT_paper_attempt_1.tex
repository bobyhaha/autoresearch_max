\begin{abstract}
We consolidate the campaign artifacts formerly numbered 019--022 and report
the result that those preregistrations could not contain prospectively.
Historical direct-SSH evidence suggested that FA3 document isolation helped,
but lacked immutable RunRecords.  A governed reproduction subsequently
progressed from mean paired $\Delta\valbpb=-0.005416$ at $n=3$, to
$-0.005299$ at $n=6$, and $-0.0054258$ at $n=10$, with every pair helping.
This is an adoption candidate, not an adopted default.  The historical
campaign minimum, $0.927183$, is one seed-47 treatment observation rather
than a contemporaneous control.  Three independent proposal agents and a
separate critic therefore selected a dual-anchor recipe-transport experiment,
rated 87.0/100 on explicit novelty, provenance, validity, impact, reliability,
feasibility, falsifiability, and program-coherence axes.  The sole immutable
four-pair/eight-H200 wave was terminally invalid: a returning foreign process
occupied the seed-47 treatment GPU, the watchdog identified its PID and
working directory, and the arm exited 79.  The deterministic evaluator
returned \texttt{invalid} with zero admissible complete pairs.  Three complete
treatment endpoints---0.927454, 0.930124, and 0.927999---are descriptive only
and none beats 0.927183.  No replay qualification, Q2, control rebind,
adoption, or new SOTA is claimed.  The central result is methodological:
before a system can improve itself, it must observe and attribute its
failures.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Supersession, and Evidence Labels}

This ICML-format artifact replaces the individual live files formerly named
paper 019, 020, 021, and both paper-022 variants.  Their committed bytes remain
recoverable in Git history.  Paper 019 was the prospective provenance-repair
preregistration.  Paper 020 reported only the $n=3$ checkpoint and was later
made background-only because it used two rather than three independent
proposers and referred to literature records not yet present in the live
registry.  Paper 021 repaired the proposer count and provenance, reported
evidence available through $n=6$, and contained no result from its proposed
mechanisms.  Its mechanism queue became background-only for block 22.  Of the
two paper-022 drafts, \emph{Dual-Anchor Transport Before Control} was the sole
canonical execution authority; \emph{Recover the Recipe, Not the Scalar} was
a withdrawn, non-authorizing design study.

This paper is a retrospective synthesis and authorizes no new GPU work.
Repository facts, historical observations, preregistered judgments, and
post-run conclusions remain distinct:
\begin{itemize}
\item $0.927183$ is the chart and provenance anchor, not a valid control.
\item Historical direct-SSH results are quarantined priors, not RunRecords.
\item Prospective $n=3$ and $n=6$ results are promotion signals.
\item Prospective $n=10$ document isolation is an adoption candidate.
\item The control-transport wave is invalid; its complete endpoints are
descriptive and cannot be subsetted into an efficacy estimate.
\end{itemize}

\section{Frozen Scientific Frame}

All scored arms use one physical H200, \texttt{STOP\_MODE=time},
\texttt{TIME\_BUDGET=300}, a frozen split, tokenizer and evaluator, FA3,
and \texttt{max-autotune-no-cudagraphs}.  Controls are concurrent,
same-seed, and physically disjoint.  Raw treatment-minus-control endpoint BPB
is authoritative.  The campaign's effective standard deviation
$\sigma=0.001198$ fixes a raw minimum-effect gate
\[
                 2\sigma=0.002396.
\]
Steps, tokens, MFU, and token-law residuals are diagnostics unless a
preregistered fidelity estimand explicitly says otherwise.  Moving compilation,
packing, prefetch, or queue priming outside the charged clock is forbidden.

Every idea is scored from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) on novelty (N), primary
provenance (P), causal validity (V), expected local impact (I), transfer
reliability (R), feasibility/cost (F), and numeric falsifiability (X).
Canonical paper 022 additionally scores program coherence (C).  Prestige can
raise provenance; it cannot override arithmetic, implementation, dependency,
fairness, or reward-hack vetoes.

\subsection{Why rank 1, and why it was plausible}

Rank 1 simultaneously asked whether the historical recipe transported to
instrumented current source and whether it beat a concurrent current recipe.
Rank 2 could test present efficacy but not historical transport.  Rank 3 was
more attributive in principle, but the repository could not atomically govern
two executable source hashes, four cells, and adequate replication.  Ranks 4
and 6 failed the same multi-source/source-launch dependency.  Rank 5
decomposed a sub-gate historical schedule contrast before qualifying the
whole operating point.  Rank 7 calibrated harness noise instead of improving
the model; rank 8 was seed-47 pseudoreplication.

The causal hypothesis was connected and deliberately local.  A smaller
global batch yields more optimizer updates at comparable token exposure;
TTTL changes attention allocation; LR, warmdown, and Muon momentum retune the
finite-budget trajectory.  The preserved recipe averaged 2002.75 optimizer
steps and 295.318M tokens.  Its point prediction had 0.0044825 BPB margin
beyond the gate.  No 2025--2026 paper establishes this exact bundle or
predicts 0.927183.  Recursive supports realistic comparator discipline and
multi-seed experimentation \cite{recursive2026}; Kimi K3 motivates
architecture--optimization--systems co-design, not transfer of this bundle's
effect \cite{kimik3}.  Conditioned initialization and gated attention support
later branches, not the recipe itself \cite{saratchandran2026conditioned,
qiu2025gated}.

\section{The Canonical Q1 Experiment}

Four current-source pairs used seeds 47--50 and all eight H200s.  Both arms
shared the frozen source and common environment.  The current control used
SSSL/.03/128/262144/.95/momentum-continuity 0; the recovered recipe used
TTTL/.04/72/147456/.85/continuity 1.  The decision required the intersection
of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a 90\% replay-equivalence interval inside
$[-0.002396,+0.002396]$;
\item a one-sided 95\% upper bound for treatment-minus-control at or below
$-0.002396$, with all $4/4$ pairs helping;
\item a 90\% log-token-ratio interval inside
$[\log(0.98),\log(1.02)]$; and
\item every source, config, runtime, device, PID, boundary, completion, and
co-tenancy invariant.
\end{enumerate}
Equivalence testing placed non-equivalence in the null; nonsignificance could
not be relabeled as equivalence \cite{schuirmann1987,lakens2017}.  Exact
six-decimal reproduction was explicitly rejected as a reward-hack surface.

\section{Connected Improvement Program}

The campaign should not respond by accumulating unrelated additions.
The dependency body is:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\boxed{\text{observable, attributable control}}\\[-2pt]
\downarrow\\[-2pt]
\boxed{\text{representation and memory}} \rightarrow
\boxed{\text{attention}}\\[-2pt]
\downarrow\hspace{75pt}\downarrow\\[-2pt]
\boxed{\text{optimization stability}} \rightarrow
\boxed{\text{systems exposure}} .
\end{gathered}
\]
Representation and attention share projection/value paths; attention and
normalization shape residual transport; optimizer mechanisms share parameter
groups; packer and producer mechanisms share loader work; systems speedups
change update exposure under a wall-clock budget.  Each mechanism requires
observable mediators---branch norms, router entropy, gradient flow, kernel
share, queue wait, or token/update exposure---so an endpoint failure can be
attributed.  Independent wins must precede a new prospective $2\times2$
interaction paper.  A plateau triggers a broad, primary-literature-grounded
search across representation, attention, memory, initialization,
normalization, data order, optimization, and systems, followed by independent
criticism; it does not license nearby knob repetition.

\section{Limitations}

The document-isolation effect is end-to-end and does not identify its
training, evaluation, or throughput submechanisms.  The recipe bundle changes
six coordinates, so even a valid transport result would not attribute
component effects.  Four historical overlap seeds provide low-power
equivalence evidence.  A clean prelaunch inventory cannot guarantee that a
tenant will not return later.  The fixed 300-second frame rewards useful
wall-clock co-design but requires unusually strict clock placement and
exposure accounting.  Finally, ratings are explicit judgments, not empirical
effects; high scores never substitute for a successful offline gate or
prospective result.

\section{Conclusion}

The campaign repaired one historical mechanism prospectively, reconstructed
the recipe behind its best scalar, ranked control-transport designs through
independent proposal and criticism, and then refused to overclaim when the
selected wave lost isolation.  The outcome is not a new SOTA.  It is evidence
that the research system can preserve a negative operational fact, identify
its cause, block invalid inference, and update its program coherently.
Observation and attribution are not administrative overhead: they are
preconditions for self-improvement.
