## Abstract

We report the hypothesis block for rounds 70+ of the `walltime_5min_h200` challenge (minimize `val_bpb` after 300 s on one H200; **reference baseline 1.0301** naive fresh-Karpathy, current SOTA **0.9506** mean / **0.9483** best (n=10), single-seed σ ≈ 0.0015, 2σ adoption gate ≈ 0.003). The prior 69-round campaign did **not** merely exhaust the cheap-lever frontier — it *diagnosed why the frontier is walled*: **the floor is data-limited, not architecture-limited.** Three findings anchor this block. (i) A data-scaling ablation shows a −0.118 `val_bpb` swing from 5→10 training shards — larger than the entire +0.082 from 64 rounds of architecture search — at constant step count; the model trains on 10 of 6543 shards (~2 epochs) and sits on the overfitting boundary. (ii) The n-gram hash memory is a **memorizer** and the *sole* carrier of that data-sensitivity: with it off, `val_bpb` is flat across data (range 0.004); with it on, range 0.118; it helps −0.029 at 10 shards but **harms +0.086 at 5 shards**, and its optimal table size is data-dependent. (iii) *Every* structural "subtraction" lever (FFN 6×→4×, span halving) **inverts with data** — it wins only at the 2-epoch operating point. The unifying thesis for this block: since capacity/subtraction levers are operating-point artifacts and more data is infrastructurally blocked (offline node), the real leverage is in **(a) fixed-inductive-bias convergence levers with ~zero memorizing capacity** (which should *not* invert with data), **(b) the never-written fused n-gram-VE kernel + an FA3→FA4 upgrade** to raise the step budget, and **(c) regularizing the memorizer** so it generalizes at ~2 epochs. Every hypothesis carries a **data-invariance falsifier** (run at 8 *and* 10 shards) — the discriminator between a genuine convergence/generalization win and another epoch-trade.

## 1. Introduction

The 5-minute frame rewards quality-per-step and steps-per-300s at once. On such a target most levers fail, and in this campaign they did — but the informative result is *structural*: profiling and a late data-scaling ablation showed the frontier is walled by **data**, not architecture. The correct response, per the project's own literature-synthesis discipline ("do not spend refinement slots on nearby knobs; on saturation, explore new mechanisms"), is to stop turning capacity knobs (whose verdicts we now know invert with data) and attack the three things that can actually move a data-limited, memorization-driven, underfit-per-step model.

We generated candidates with the protocol's multi-agent procedure: **Proposer A** (convergence/architecture), **Proposer B** (systems/throughput/kernel), **Proposer C** (data-efficiency/memorizer-regularization), and a **Critic** that adversarially ranks the pooled slate. The coordinator verifies every proposer claim against the actual SOTA code (`research/sota_snapshots/sota_swin4/train.py`) before belief.

## 2. Related Work

- **Canon layers** — Allen-Zhu et al., *Physics of Language Models Part 4.1* (2025): inserting cheap zero-init causal depthwise convolutions that sum a few neighboring token *hidden states* improves loss and reasoning depth at negligible params/compute. Motivates **canon_conv** — a differentiable feature transform, *orthogonal* to the discrete n-gram memorizer.
- **Differential Transformer** — Ye et al. (2024): `softmax(A1) − λ·softmax(A2)` cancels common-mode attention noise, improving LM perplexity at matched size. Motivates **diff_attention**; unusually cheap here because attention is only ~5% of our step time.
- **Multi-Scale Window Attention (MSWA)** — (2501.01039): diverse window sizes across heads/layers beat uniform SWA at equal cost (~12.5% lower time/memory). Motivates **mswa** (flagged for span-inversion risk).
- **FlashAttention** — Dao et al. (2022) and **FA4** (2025, per Recursive's report of a large gain on a comparable nanochat/nanoGPT frame). Motivates **fa4_upgrade** — an attention-kernel refinement.
- **Data Movement Is All You Need** — Ivanov, Ben-Nun et al. (2021): fusing elementwise/index ops removes the launch + memory-traffic overhead that dominates non-GEMM time. Motivates **fused-vehash** — the fused n-gram index→gather kernel.
- **Muon NS acceleration** — Turbo-Muon / Chebyshev-type Newton-Schulz (2506.10935, 2606.27216); *How Much Orthogonalization Does Muon Need?* (2606.00371): comparable orthogonalization in ~10 vs 15 matmuls. Motivates **muon_ns_reduce** (secondary; optimizer is not the bottleneck).
- **Scaling Data-Constrained Language Models** — Muennighoff et al. (2023, arXiv 2305.16264): up to ~4 epochs of repeated data ≈ as good as fresh; beyond, the value of repetition decays. **Scope tension to assess:** they find 4 epochs fine at larger scale; we overfit at 3 — a scope-mismatch that bounds how their prescription transfers to our 50M/10-shard frame.
- **Tensorizing Engram** (2606.08347) and **Scaling Embeddings > Scaling Experts** (2601.21204): sharing latents across n-gram embedding tables is beneficial; scaling explicit n-gram memory beats scaling MoE experts. Motivates the **memorizer-regularization** track (Proposer C, pending).

## 3. Method — hypotheses and five-dimension scoring

Scores are 1–10 on **novelty, validity, impact, reliability, provenance**. Every lever carries a **data-invariance falsifier**: a win at 10 shards that shrinks or inverts at 8 shards is an epoch-trade, not a convergence/generalization win, and is rejected.

### 3.1 canon_conv — zero-init causal depthwise conv in the residual stream (explore, architecture)
**Mechanism.** Add `x = x + DWConv_causal(norm(x))` per Block: a zero-initialized depthwise 1-D conv (kernel 3–4) over token hidden states (~3k params/site). It gives each position cheap learned local composition of its predecessors' hidden states — a differentiable feature transform *orthogonal* to the n-gram lookup memorizer, adding no addressable memory to overfit. Zero-init ⇒ strict no-op at step 0 (worst case neutral); it can only be pulled in if it lowers train loss. Attacks the genuine per-step underfit.
**Prediction / falsifier.** −0.004…−0.008 `val_bpb` at 10 shards, steps within ~1%. **Falsifier:** over n≥3, mean < 0.003 gate, OR the win shrinks/inverts at 8 shards, OR conv costs >3% steps.
**Scores.** novelty 6 · validity 8 · impact 6 · reliability 8 · provenance 9.
**Code site.** `Block.__init__`/`forward` (~L131/L136); route the 3-D conv weight to a **new AdamW group** (it breaks `muon_step_fused`'s 2-D assumption) in `setup_optimizer`.

### 3.2 fused-vehash — fused n-gram index→gather→sum kernel (explore→refine, systems_kernel) — TOP KERNEL PRIORITY
**Mechanism.** The measured ~194k tiny fill/int kernels come from `ngram_hash_ids` (a fresh `torch.zeros` + slice-assign per shift, per order, every step) plus two `2^21×kv_dim` gathers. Indices are a **pure function of tokens + fixed primes (zero weight dependence)** ⇒ fusible byte-identically. Two realizations: **(B1)** wrap the hash + accumulation in one `@torch.compile(fullgraph=True)` region so Inductor fuses the int64 chain and removes the intermediate allocations (cheap first cut); **(B2)** a single Triton kernel doing hash→both-gathers→sum in 1–2 launches, *or* precompute indices in the async CPU dataloader / bake `hash_ids.bin` offline (removes the construction from the GPU critical path entirely). This is the fused kernel the campaign diagnosed but never wrote.
**Prediction / verification.** B1 +150…+350 steps; B2 +250…+500 steps. **Byte-identical check:** `torch.equal(old_ids, new_ids)` (integer-exact) + fixed-order `ng` sum + a fixed-step (200-step, same-seed) loss reproduce bit-for-bit (modulo fp-reassoc ε). **Falsifier:** any index mismatch, step-time doesn't drop, or fixed-step loss diverges beyond fp-reassoc noise; for the loader variant, loader-wait becomes the new bottleneck.
**Scores.** B1 novelty 5 · validity 9 · impact 7 · reliability 8 · provenance 9. B2 novelty 7 · validity 9 · impact 8 · reliability 6 · provenance 9.
**Code site.** `ngram_hash_ids` (L55–67) + `forward` ng-construction (L302–306).

### 3.3 attention-kernel/precision upgrade — FA4 + fp8-attention re-test (refine, systems_kernel) — OPERATOR-ELEVATED, unmasked after n-gram fusion
**Provenance correction (honest).** A re-fetch of the Recursive blog shows the big attention lever they report is **"FP8 attention projections"** (fwd `float8_e4m3`, bwd bf16 — "pushed FP8 into the attention path"), a major contributor on the NanoGPT Speedrun; the retrievable text does not name "FA4" explicitly (it may be an unindexed detail, or the recalled "big jump" is this fp8-attention lever). **This directly bears on our campaign: we *retracted* fp8 as a contention artifact — but Recursive made fp8-attention a real win, so our retraction may have been premature on the noisy shared cluster.** The operator explicitly overrides the critic's demotion; this lever is re-elevated.
**Mechanism.** Two coupled moves: (a) upgrade `ATTN_BACKEND=fa3`→**FA4** if available (Hopper warp-specialization/pipelining/softmax scheduling → higher attention throughput + MFU); (b) **fp8 attention projections** done right (Q/K/V and/or PV in `float8_e4m3` fwd, bf16 bwd), re-tested with the mandatory GPU-controlled paired design that the original retraction lacked.
**The masking/unmasking argument (reconciles operator vs critic).** Attention is only ~5% of step time *now* because the n-gram-VE path dominates (launch-bound). So an attention-kernel win is **masked until B1/B2 fuse that sink** — after fusion, attention's share of the (smaller) step grows and the same kernel/precision upgrade yields a *larger* wall-clock gain. Hence this lever is **sequenced right after B1 + a fresh nsys re-profile**, not run first.
**Prediction / falsifier.** Before fusion: <2% step gain (masked). After fusion: re-profile determines attention's new share; PASS if attention-kernel/fp8 gives ≥ +5% steps at fixed quality (fp8 fixed-step quality within σ). **Falsifier:** fp8 fixed-step loss degrades > σ (numerics), or post-fusion attention share still <10% (kernel not the lever). Feasibility gate: FA4 package on H200, drop-in at `fa3.flash_attn_func`, sliding-window + causal support; fp8 via `torch._scaled_mm` already available.
**Scores.** novelty 6 · validity 7 · impact 7 (raised: fp8-attention was a *real* Recursive lever + unmasked post-fusion) · reliability 6 · provenance 8 (Recursive fp8-attention + Dao lineage).
**Code site.** attention backend selection + `CausalSelfAttention.forward` (L95–114); fp8 projections at the q/k/v `c_*` linears + PV.

### 3.4 diff_attention — differential (common-mode-cancelling) attention (refine, attention)
**Mechanism.** `out = softmax(Q1K1ᵀ)V − λ·softmax(Q2K2ᵀ)V` + sublayer RMSNorm; cancels common-mode attention noise → cleaner gradients → faster convergence. Param-neutral, changes attention *quality* not memory capacity (should not invert with data). Two FA3 calls sharing `v` — nearly free given attention is ~5% of step time.
**Prediction / falsifier.** −0.003…−0.006 at 10 shards, steps within ~2%. **Falsifier:** mean < 0.003 over n≥3, OR steps drop >3% (attention was binding), OR inverts at 8 shards, OR win only appears after per-config λ tuning (fragile).
**Scores.** novelty 7 · validity 7 · impact 6 · reliability 5 · provenance 8.
**Code site.** `CausalSelfAttention.forward` (L95–114); λ param → AdamW scalar group.

### 3.5 memorizer-regularization track (explore/refine, data-efficiency) — the on-thesis track
Regularize/restructure the n-gram memorizer so it generalizes at ~2 epochs, reducing the +0.086 harm at 5 shards. **Shared premise:** the harm is concentrated in *low-count / high-magnitude rare* n-gram keys whose memorized correction is shard-specific noise. **Shared falsifier/deliverable:** the {5,8,10}-shard *slope* — a genuine memorizer-fix helps *more* as data shrinks (unlike operating-point-specific subtraction levers). All three preserve coverage (unlike shrinking the table to 2^15, which buys less capacity by *forcing collisions*).

- **C1 — table-only decoupled weight decay** (refine). Strong decoupled WD on `ngram_embeds` alone (own AdamW group), sweep λ∈{0.05,0.2,0.5,1.0}. Every row is pulled toward zero every step; only rows hit that step get gradient away from zero, so frequent/consistent n-grams keep a large correction and rare/noisy ones back off to the backbone — Good-Turing discounting as continuous row-shrinkage. Pred: big help @5sh (+0.086 collapses ≥0.02), neutral-to-slight @10sh. nov5/val8/imp6/rel8/prov7. **Load-bearing caveat:** if `NGRAM_SPARSE_GRAD` is active, AdamW may only decay *touched* rows — must force a dense full-table decay (`p.mul_(1−lr·λ)`) or the mechanism doesn't hold. Cheapest to ship. Code: split optimizer group at train.py:269/282.
- **C2 — factorized/composite n-gram codes** (explore, *Tensorizing Engram* 2606.08347). Replace each 2^21×d table with a product-code: hash each key into C sub-codes indexing small shared codebooks (K=2^12), embedding = sum of C rows. Free params ↓~256× yet all 2^21 keys keep *distinct* composite vectors (coverage preserved). **The one lever that could raise the scored 10-shard operating point** (keep the coverage win, shed the residual 2-epoch overfitting). Pred: approach 2^15 @5sh AND stay within ~0.002 of 2^21 @10sh. nov8/val7/imp8/rel5/prov8. Biggest upside/risk (touches forward 302–306, module 163, init 192–193, max-autotune + sparse-grad). **Two-sided falsifier:** regresses >0.003 vs 2^21 @10sh OR fails to beat plain 2^15 @5sh → reject.
- **C3 — frequency-confidence backoff gate** (refine→explore, neural Good-Turing). Scale each key's contribution `ng *= f(count(key))`, `f(c)=c/(c+κ)`, from a cheap int count buffer (one scatter-add prepass over the 10 shards, ~4 MB/order). **Intrinsically data-adaptive** (counts lower at 5 shards → the gate closes harder exactly when memorization is most dangerous, no retuning). Pred: monotone harm reduction, largest @5sh. nov7/val8/imp7/rel5/prov8. **Highest-information experiment** — its falsifier directly tests the shared premise of C1/C2, so **run it first**; a null down-weights the whole family. Risk: the count-buffer prepass sits outside the compiled graph. Code: `register_buffer` count filled before the loop; apply at forward 304–306 before the per-head `ve_gate`.

### 3.6 Cut / deprioritized (recorded for provenance)
- **muon_ns_reduce** (ns_steps 5→3 / 10-matmul schedule): optimizer is *not* the bottleneck (NS runs on tiny 768-dim batched+compiled matmuls); not byte-identical, so it spends scarce quality-equivalence budget for a small win. Only after fused-vehash lands.
- **mswa** (graded windows): adjacent to the *known* span-inversion lever; keep only as a cheap probe with a decisive 8-shard invariance check; low priority.
- **sparse-grad / fp8 / CUDA-graphs**: already measured dead (sparse-grad regressed throughput on torch 2.9; fp8 was a retracted contention artifact — launch-bound not compute-bound; CUDA graphs segfault with max-autotune).

## 4. Experimental setup

Frame `walltime_5min_h200`: `STOP_MODE=time`, `TIME_BUDGET=300`, one H200, `ATTN_BACKEND=fa3`(→fa4 under test), `COMPILE_MODE=max-autotune-no-cudagraphs`. Reference baseline **1.0301**; current SOTA **0.9506** (n=10, σ≈0.0015); 2σ gate ≈ 0.003; single-seed band ±0.0015. GPU-controlled paired same-seed A/B with a fresh concurrent control (shared cluster; interleave/swap arms — an fp8 "win" was once a placement artifact). Each lever env-flag gated, byte-identical when off; stage-1 one-pair discovery screen → escalate to n≥3 only on a signal past the floor.

**Three mandatory rigor protocols this block:**
1. **Data-invariance check (quality levers).** Any adopted lever is re-run at **8 shards**; if its 10-shard win shrinks/inverts, it is an epoch-trade and is rejected. This is the discriminator the prior campaign lacked until round 65.
2. **Two-run throughput protocol (kernel levers).** (a) fixed-step (200-step, same-seed) A/B must reproduce loss bit-for-bit (byte-identity); (b) fixed-300s run measures whether the extra steps actually lower `val_bpb` or just buy more epochs at the 2-epoch boundary. If (a) identical but (b) flat/worse, the throughput is real but redirect it into *quality/unique-tokens*, not more epochs.
3. **Decouple mechanism-quality from naive-kernel maturity (architectural levers).** A new architectural component (Canon conv, factorized codes, differential attention) ships with an *unrefined* kernel — its first-pass step-time will often regress simply because the op isn't fused/tuned, not because the mechanism is bad. So a new lever is judged on **per-update quality first**: run a **fixed-step (equal-#updates) A/B** to isolate whether the mechanism lowers loss per update, held apart from wall-clock. Decision rule: (i) if fixed-step quality is **null** → reject the mechanism (kernel refinement can't save a lever that doesn't help per update); (ii) if fixed-step quality **clears the gate** but the naive kernel regresses the 300s step budget → the lever is **promoted to a kernel-refinement task** (fuse/tune its op, per the B1/B2 methodology), *not* rejected — only after refinement does it face the final fixed-300s verdict. This prevents discarding a real inductive-bias win on account of a slow first kernel (the failure mode the operator flagged); the step-time conditions in the gates below are therefore *refinement triggers*, not kill conditions, whenever fixed-step quality is positive.

Direction rotation satisfied (architecture / systems_kernel / attention / data-efficiency are distinct). Explore:refine ≈ 6:4 honored.

## 5. Results / analysis to date (the 69-round campaign)

- **Architecture floor certified** (+7.9%, six levers) then exhausted: every axis (n-gram order/table/skip/bag, attention span/pattern/heads/GQA, positional, init, optimizer, depth, regularization) mapped and mechanistically explained.
- **Floor is data-limited**: data-scaling −0.118 (5→10 shards) ≫ +0.082 (64 rounds). All subtraction levers invert with data.
- **N-gram memory is the memorizer**: sole carrier of data-sensitivity; optimal table size data-dependent.
- **Kernel bottleneck measured but never fixed**: n-gram-VE path = ~194k tiny kernels, launch-bound, MFU ~19%; attention ~5%, CE ~1.6%. fp8/sparse-grad/CUDA-graphs dead. **This block finally writes the fused kernel.**

## 6. Discussion

The block is internally consistent with the two measured facts — **underfit per-step** and **data-limited/memorization-driven**. `canon_conv` and `diff_attention` attack per-step convergence with fixed inductive bias that should *not* invert with data; `fused-vehash` (+`fa4_upgrade`) attacks the verifiable launch-overhead sink to widen the step budget every quality lever competes in; the memorizer-regularization track attacks the actual binding constraint (data-fragility of the memorizer). The highest-certainty value is `fused-vehash` B1 (byte-identical, compounding, hits the exact measured bottleneck); the highest-*thesis* value is the memorizer track (it targets the −0.118 data-sensitivity directly). The largest uncertainty is the **throughput→val_bpb conversion** at the 2-epoch boundary — hence the two-run protocol.

## 7. Critic pass — cuts and final ranking

The adversarial critic cut **4** candidates: **A2 diff_attention** (attention gains are a long-context/large-scale phenomenon; at 50M/short-context they routinely fail, and it tunes a path that is ~5% of step time — predicted effect sits *at* 2σ); **A3 mswa** (sub-gate lower bound and adjacent to the *known span-inversion* lever → not data-robust); **B3 muon_ns_reduce** (optimizer is not the bottleneck and it is *not* byte-identical — degrades orthogonalization to buy throughput in the wrong place); **FA4** was initially demoted by the critic (attention ~5% of step time → capped EV) — **but the operator overrode this** with evidence that an attention-precision/kernel upgrade gave Recursive a big jump (their reported lever is *fp8 attention projections*; §3.3). The override is mechanistically sound via the **masking argument**: attention is only ~5% *because* the n-gram-VE sink dominates, so the attention lever is unmasked once B1/B2 fuse that sink. It is therefore **re-elevated and sequenced after B1 + a fresh re-profile**, and paired with a rigorous re-test of fp8-attention (which we previously retracted, possibly prematurely). Not a scored run *before* fusion (it would read null, masked); a real scored lever *after*.

**Single highest-value experiment: C2 (factorized n-gram codes)** — the only lever that can move the *scored 10-shard SOTA* and the data-robustness thesis simultaneously. **Single riskiest: B2** — a hand-written Triton kernel whose entire premise B1 can cheaply invalidate; hence B1 gates B2.
