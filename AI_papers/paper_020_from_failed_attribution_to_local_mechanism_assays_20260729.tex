\section{Connected Thesis and Attribution Rule}

The model already combines full in-document attention with token and
hashed n-gram values.  Its plateau can therefore arise even when useful
features exist: a residual shortcut may dominate deeper transformations,
multiple hash heads may alias the same contexts together, or an attention
branch may begin in a poorly conditioned or badly scaled state.  The proposed
body is
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{gradient-preserving residual depth}\\
\Downarrow\\
\text{independent conditional-memory evidence}\\
\Downarrow\\
\text{conditioned and balanced attention injection}.
\end{gathered}
\]
This is a connected cumulative program, not permission to bundle additions.
Each treatment differs from its current comparator by exactly one mechanism.
A mechanism joins the comparator only after the full preregistered acceptance
funnel, not after an offline pass, a diagnostic, or one paired screen.
Rejection, invalidity, or a failed mediator leaves the comparator unchanged.
A conditional child cannot execute unless its named parent was accepted.

The governing principle is:
\begin{quote}
\emph{Before a system can improve itself, it must be able to observe and
attribute its failures.}
\end{quote}
Consequently, an observed endpoint without its predicted mediator is evidence
against the stated explanation, even when numerically favorable.  It is not
silently relabeled as a success.

\paragraph{Why GPAS is ranked first.}
An initial portfolio critic scored GPAS
4/5/4/3/3/5/5/5 = 34/40.  The deeper source/code transfer audit lowered
reliability by one point, producing the authoritative 33/40 shown above.
GPAS makes an unusually sharp causal prediction.  In a Pre-LN residual
sublayer with post-sum activation $z$, it applies
\[
z' = z-\silu(\alpha_\ell)\sg(z).
\]
The forward scale is $1-\silu(\alpha_\ell)$, while
$\partial z'/\partial z=I$.  The official implementation uses one
zero-initialized scalar per layer and reuses it after both the attention and
FFN residual sums \cite{gpas2025,gpascode}.  Across 71M, 130M, 250M, 350M,
and 1B Pre-LN models, the paper reports evaluation perplexity changes
33.98$\rightarrow$33.38, 26.61$\rightarrow$26.25,
21.54$\rightarrow$21.34, 20.71$\rightarrow$19.77, and
16.53$\rightarrow$16.11.  More importantly for attribution, the 350M
ablation reports 20.35 with stop-gradient, 21.34 without it, and 21.35 with no
GPAS, while forward variance is still reduced without stop-gradient.
This isolates gradient preservation from mere downscaling.

The selection is still a transfer hypothesis, not a replication claim.
The source reports no error bars or multi-seed significance, and its models
are 12 layers or deeper.  OPHIS has eight layers, learned residual and $x_0$
paths, per-head Q/K normalization and gating, normalized MLP output,
zero-initialized $c_{\rm proj}$ weights, Muon/AdamW/RMSProp optimizer
partitioning, and hashed value memory.  Those differences lower the critic's
impact and reliability scores and make the baseline applicability mediator
mandatory.

\section{Round 1: Source-Faithful GPAS}

\paragraph{Intervention.}
Add one parameter $\alpha_\ell=0$ for each of eight layers.  Reuse that
scalar after both the attention and MLP residual sums, exactly as in the
official implementation.  The gate joins the same local AdamW class used for
learned residual scalars: paper-frozen learning rate 0.005, betas
$(0.8,0.95)$, epsilon $10^{-10}$, no weight decay, and no momentum-decay
flag.  This value is a constant, not a derivation from current $R_0$'s
\texttt{scalar\_lr}$=0.8$ (the ratio is 0.00625).  This optimizer mapping is
an OPHIS-specific extrapolation; the source uses Adam at $5\times10^{-4}$ for
all parameters.  This wording was corrected prospectively before any
Round-1 GPU execution or result.

\paragraph{Zero-effect replay.}
Before a mechanism assay, one physical H200 runs same-device control replay
and an $\alpha=0$ treatment replay from separately constructed but
source-identical states.  At step 0, logits, loss, all pre-existing parameter
gradients, data IDs/targets/bytes, packer boundaries, RNG identity, and
compiler identity must match exactly.  The new gate gradients must be finite
and nonzero.  All \texttt{PACKER\_*} diagnostic flags are zero.  A mismatch is
\texttt{INVALID\_DIAGNOSTIC}; it cannot be repaired by relaxing equality
after seeing the value.

\paragraph{Applicability mediator.}
The first execution after replay is one eager, training-only 256-step
treatment/control mediator pair; residual probes require compilation to be
disabled.  Its control rows 1--32 define post-MLP residual variance at layer
$\ell$ as
\[
v_{\ell,t} =
\operatorname*{mean}_{b,s}
\operatorname*{Var}_{d}\!\left[z_{\ell,t}(b,s,d)\right],
\]
computed in FP64 outside the scored endpoint.  In at least 24 of 32 control
steps, Spearman correlation between layer index and $\log v_{\ell,t}$ must be
at least 0.60 and $v_{7,t}/v_{0,t}\ge1.25$.  Otherwise the local model lacks
the source mechanism and the round is killed before endpoint work.
Post-attention statistics are recorded but cannot replace the registered
post-MLP statistic.

\paragraph{Treatment mediation and safety.}
Across that eager 256-step pair, the treatment/control ratio of
$v_7/v_0$ must be at most 0.85 and the treatment's deepest-layer variance
must be at most 0.90 of control.  Setting the learned gates to zero at the
assay endpoint must restore at least 50\% of the variance-ratio gap, tying the
effect to GPAS rather than incidental drift.  Every
$1-\silu(\alpha_\ell)$ must stay finite in $[0.5,1.5]$; all gate and
pre-existing gradients must remain finite; and the deepest four layers must
contribute at least half of the aggregate variance-ratio reduction.

Only after the eager pair passes, two separate no-probe compiled 256-step
pairs run on the same two frozen H200 UUIDs with roles swapped.  Every arm in
each placement must have exactly one Dynamo graph, zero recompiles, and zero
CUDA-graph recordings; timing uses steps 33--256.  The counterbalanced
treatment/control token-rate point ratio must be at least 0.99 with a
one-sided 95\% lower bound of at least 0.985.  Each arm's clean steps 33--256
are partitioned into 28 ordered, nonoverlapping eight-step blocks.  With
\texttt{random.Random(19019063)}, each of 10,000 replicates draws 28 block
indices with replacement independently per placement, reusing that placement's
index vector for its control and treatment, and takes the geometric mean of
the two aggregate treatment/control token-rate ratios.  The lower bound is
ascending order statistic 499 (zero based); any missing, nonfinite, or
nonpositive block or replicate invalidates the diagnostic.  This bootstrap
specification was corrected prospectively before any Round-1 GPU execution.
Thus one registered seed/pair
has exactly three governed pair executions---one eager mediator and two clean
timing placements---not three endpoint-funnel pairs.  Replay is judged before
the eager pair, mediation immediately afterward, and later phases are skipped
on any failure.  This separation was corrected prospectively before any
Round-1 GPU execution.  A pass authorizes only drafting and registering a
separate ordinary paired endpoint proposal.  The predicted raw paired endpoint effect is
$\Delta\bpb\le-0.0030$; ordinary promotion still requires the registered
$-0.002396$ magnitude and confidence gates.

\section{Rounds 2--3: Hash Independence}

\paragraph{Source and local extrapolation.}
Engram uses layer/head-specific odd mixers and distinct prime table moduli
\cite{engram2026,engramcode}.  OPHIS already uses disjoint multiplier tuples
but applies the same composite modulus
$8192\times64=524288$ to all fourteen active bigram/trigram tables.
Round 2 changes only these moduli while preserving raw token IDs;
canonicalized IDs are forbidden.  Prime bracketing around 524288 is a local
constant-sum extrapolation, not an Engram result.

\paragraph{Distinct-prime reserve: definition v1 withdrawn.}
The proposer score was 35/40 and the earlier critic score was 31/40.  After
implementation audit, the authoritative critic vector is
4/4/2/2/2/3/2/5 = 24/40, with verdict \holdx{}.  The first proposed capsule
serialized a width of 256 and claimed
1,879,048,192 active memory parameters.  Direct inspection instead gives
\texttt{n\_embd}=768, six KV heads, \texttt{kv\_dim}=768, and a
$K=2$ half-width of 384, so the same 7,340,032 rows contain
2,818,572,288 active parameters.  Its stated canonical payload was also
not independently reproducible from the initial stated serialization.
These are prospective design failures, not experiment results.  V1 is
\texttt{WITHDRAWN\_INVALID\_SPECIFICATION}; no collision, throughput, BPB,
adoption, or mechanism conclusion may be drawn from it.

Round 2 remains a research slot but has no execution authority until a
separately versioned, append-only v2 is frozen.  V2 must bind exact canonical
JSON bytes and an independently reproduced digest; live source and config
hashes; the complete fourteen-table inventory; runtime-derived width;
parameter membership, shapes, dtypes, counts, and bytes; and the actual
optimizer state keys, tensor shapes, dtypes, counts, and bytes under the bound
\texttt{NGRAM\_STATE\_ROWWISE} and \texttt{NGRAM\_SPARSE\_GRAD} flags.
Arithmetic equality alone is insufficient.  V1 remains preserved and cannot
be overwritten or used as v2 evidence.  If v2 is not frozen when this slot is
reached, Round 2 is skipped and Round 3 remains blocked.  The replacement may
not alter the following metrics after observing corpus values.

\paragraph{Frozen offline census.}
Aggregate raw n-gram frequency counters from exactly eight pinned training
shards; validation data are forbidden.  For every order $n$, layer $\ell$,
head $k$, and unordered distinct key pair $g<g'$, let $C_{n\ell k}(g,g')$
indicate a bucket collision and weight it by $f_gf_{g'}$.  The double
collision is $J_{n\ell}=C_{n\ell0}C_{n\ell1}$.  The authoritative aggregate
collision lift is
\[
L_n^w =
\frac{Q_n^w\sum_\ell D_{n\ell}^w}
{\sum_\ell B_{n\ell0}^w B_{n\ell1}^w},
\quad
Q_n^w=\sum_{g<g'}f_gf_{g'}.
\]
The control premise requires unweighted expected support at least 100 and a
weighted lift point estimate and one-sided 95\% bootstrap lower bound at
least 1.25.  The treatment must have lift at most 1.10 and at most 0.80 of
control; weighted double-collision burden must be at most 0.75 of control
with one-sided 95\% upper bound at most 0.90; unweighted burden must be at
most 0.80; and at least six of seven layer sites may not regress.

Marginal weighted collision burden must be at most 1.005 of control overall
and 1.01 in every layer.  Repeated full aliases across layer pairs must be at
most 0.85 of control.  Occurrence-weighted R\'enyi-2 joint entropy deficit
must be at most 0.80 of control, while no individual marginal entropy may
fall by more than $5\times10^{-4}$.  The paired bootstrap uses whole-shard
resampling, seed 19019063, 10,000 replicates, and the same eight-index vector
for both arms.  Zero denominators or any non-finite replicate fail closed.
This offline pass is necessary but never sufficient for adoption.

The runtime treatment must preserve exact total embedding parameters,
optimizer-state elements, data, targets, byte counts, ordering, BOS
positions, boundaries, non-memory initialization, and global RNG.  It must
compile to one graph with zero recompiles and slow charged token rate by at
most 0.5\%.  Its predicted endpoint effect is
$\Delta\bpb\le-0.0030$.

\paragraph{Conditional $K=4$ child.}
Round 3 is eligible only after full Round-2 acceptance.  It uses four
distinct prime tables per injection, each with one quarter of the accepted
$K=2$ concatenated width.  Total embedding parameters and every RMSProp state
element must match exactly; initial concatenated memory-output variance must
be within 2\%; non-memory parameters, data, and RNG must be exact.  Its joint
collision burden must be at most 0.75 of the accepted $K=2$ comparator on the
same census, graph count must be unchanged, and slowdown must be at most
1.0\%.  The predicted paired effect is
$\Delta\bpb\le-0.0026$.  Engram uses eight hash heads; $K=4$ is a local
intermediate, not source-faithful replication.

\section{Rounds 4--5: Attention Transport}

\paragraph{Conditioned Q/K/V initialization.}
Round 4 uses the ICLR 2026 paper's joint construction: independent
semi-orthogonal per-head Q/K maps and rectangular-identity V maps
\cite{conditioned2026}.  It does not revive Paper 019's disjoint-V
extrapolation.  Before training, each Q/K row-orthogonality residual must be
at most $10^{-5}$, every V map must satisfy
$\sigma_{\min}/\sigma_{\max}\ge0.99$, and all non-Q/K/V tensors plus global
RNG must match control exactly.  The registered attention-Jacobian surrogate
must fall at least 20\% in every layer, while attention entropy changes by at
most 0.05.

Because OPHIS initializes $c_{\rm proj}=0$, the first backward must produce a
finite nonzero $c_{\rm proj}$ gradient, and the backward after the first
update must produce finite nonzero Q/K/V gradients.  One compiled graph and
timing within 0.1\% are required.  The prediction is
$\Delta\bpb\le-0.0030$.  The source reports TinyStories
2.39$\rightarrow$2.20 and WikiText-103
44.2$\rightarrow$42.7 at 472M parameters, but no local seed variance; the
surrogate is a bound, not the true condition number.

\paragraph{Conditional attention Peri-RMSNorm.}
Round 5 first asks whether the current accepted comparator has the proposed
problem.  Across 32 fixed diagnostic steps, the post-$c_{\rm proj}$ attention
branch must have max/min layer RMS at least 1.25 or layerwise coefficient of
variation at least 0.20.  Otherwise the round is killed offline.  If eligible,
apply a fixed scale-free RMS normalization only to the attention output after
$c_{\rm proj}$; the MLP already has an analogous normalization.  This is an
OPHIS-specific partial placement, not the full learned-scale Peri-LN
architecture \cite{periln2025}.

Step-0 attention output must remain exactly zero.  At step 1, each
$c_{\rm proj}$ gradient ratio must lie in $[0.5,2.0]$ and no layer may exceed
2.5 times control.  Across the 32-step diagnostic, residual-norm coefficient
of variation and max/min layer-gradient ratio must each fall at least 20\%.
No non-finite value is allowed; slowdown is at most 1.0\%; the predicted
paired effect is $\Delta\bpb\le-0.0030$.

\section{Falsification and Non-Claims}

The block is successful as research even if no candidate is adopted, provided
each failure is attributed to a preregistered mediator.  GPAS can fail because
depthwise variance growth is absent, the gate does not mediate it, or the
cost exceeds its limit.  Prime moduli can fail because the control has no
cross-head dependence, independence does not improve, or marginal entropy
regresses.  Conditioned initialization can fail its spectral surrogate,
gradient-onset, or endpoint prediction.  Peri-RMSNorm can fail because no
branch imbalance exists or normalization amplifies the zero-output path.

No offline metric, mechanism assay, one-pair value, historical chart point, or
paper-reported improvement is a local SOTA.  A new SOTA requires a terminal
governed result whose preregistered comparator, full funnel, evidence update,
decision, snapshot, chart point, and reproducibility code all pass.  Until
then, the best displayed value remains historical experiment 501 at
0.927183, with its original timestamp and its restricted provenance role.

\section{Conclusion}

Paper 020 replaces plateau knob-search with five falsifiable mechanism
questions.  Its first question is deliberately local: does OPHIS even exhibit
the depthwise residual-variance mechanism that GPAS is designed to correct?
Its second asks whether existing n-gram heads provide independent evidence
rather than correlated aliases, but remains blocked after the implementation
auditor caught a stale width and incomplete equality contract.  Later rounds
condition and scale the attention path only through measured applicability.
The accepted-only chain turns separate architectural ideas into one connected
body while keeping every causal increment identifiable.
