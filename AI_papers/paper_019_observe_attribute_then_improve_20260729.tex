\begin{abstract}
This paper prospectively freezes the next five rounds of a 300-second,
single-H200 language-model autoresearch campaign after a control-transport
failure and a performance plateau.  The campaign minimum, $0.927183$
validation bits per byte (BPB), remains a quarantined historical treatment
observation, not an executable control or a new evidence contribution.
Three independent proposal agents read primary 2025--2026 work across
normalization, initialization, conditional memory, data order, optimization,
and kernels.  A separate adversarial critic rescored every candidate on
novelty, provenance, causal validity, local impact, transfer reliability,
feasibility, falsifiability, and program coherence.  The resulting connected
program is: (1) exact packer-sourced FA3 boundary metadata, conditional on a
robust throughput gate; (2) canonicalized Engram hash keys; (3) conditioned
per-head Q/K initialization plus an explicitly local full-rank V adaptation;
(4) attention-output Peri-RMSNorm, conditional on gradient-safety mediators;
and (5) a pack-preserving compression curriculum.  The highest experimental
score belongs to canonicalized keys; the boundary sidecar is sequenced first
because it is exact-semantics infrastructure whose accepted gain would
increase charged update exposure for every subsequent round.  Each accepted
mechanism becomes the comparator for the next, producing one attributed model
rather than a bag of additions.  A fresh same-seed recovered-recipe
comparator is permitted only within each new causal question.  This paper
authorizes no endpoint claim by itself: every round must first clear its
offline mediator, parity, source, clock, and isolation gates.  The governing
principle is that before a system can improve itself, it must be able to
observe and attribute its failures.
\end{abstract}

\section{Status, Evidence Labels, and Non-Claims}

This is the new prospective paper 019.  It follows the retrospective
\texttt{GPT\_paper\_attempt\_1}, which superseded the former papers 019--022.
It may authorize at most five sequential research rounds after typed registry
binding, code reconciliation, and green setup checks.  It contains no new
experimental result.

The public chart starts at experiment 501 and its recorded timestamp, with
$\valbpb=0.927183$.  That scalar was produced by one historical seed-47
treatment under a recovered six-coordinate recipe.  It is a provenance and
visualization anchor only.  A later governed four-pair transport wave was
terminally invalid because a foreign process occupied one assigned GPU; the
watchdog attributed the exact PID and exited 79.  Three completed recovered
endpoints---0.927454, 0.930124, and 0.927999---are descriptive only.  Thus:
\[
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\text{no transport verdict or control rebind,}\\[-2pt]
\text{no new SOTA}
\end{gathered}}
\]
The failed wave cannot be retried, remapped, repaired, or subsetted.  The new
questions below use fresh seeds beginning at 63 and do not estimate recipe
transport.

\section{Scientific Frame and Comparator Contract}

The active challenge is one physical H200 per arm,
\texttt{STOP\_MODE=time}, \texttt{TIME\_BUDGET=300}, FA3, varlen document
isolation, a frozen tokenizer/split/evaluator, and
\texttt{max-autotune-no-cudagraphs}.  The primary estimand is raw,
same-seed, concurrent treatment-minus-comparator endpoint BPB.  The effective
standard deviation $\sigma=0.001198$ fixes the minimum useful effect
\[
                         2\sigma=0.002396.
\]
Moving compilation, packing, metadata construction, prefetch, or queue
priming outside the charged clock is forbidden unless both arms perform the
same work there and the intervention is explicitly defined as changing the
producer interface.

\paragraph{Recipe comparator.}
The within-experiment comparator is current canonical source with
\texttt{WINDOW\_PATTERN=TTTL}, device/global batch $72/147456$, matrix LR
.04, warmdown .85, continuous Muon momentum, FA3 varlen/both document
isolation, 64$\times$ n-gram tables, and every remaining frozen setting.
Fresh concurrent execution makes this a valid comparator for
\[
Y(\text{recipe}+M)-Y(\text{recipe}),
\]
where $M$ is one new mechanism.  It does \emph{not} make the recipe a
qualified global control, validate the invalid transport wave, or turn
$0.927183$ into a contemporaneous observation.

\section{Multiagent Generation and Independent Critique}

Architecture, memory/optimization/data, and systems/kernel agents generated
frozen slates independently.  They could inspect the repository and primary
sources but could not edit the live experiment graph.  A fourth agent then
checked source faithfulness, local code fit, arithmetic, causal contrasts,
mediator observability, novelty duplication, dependencies, clock placement,
and reward-hack surfaces.  The critic was told that the ordinary governed
scheduler executes exactly two roles per experiment; proposed four-arm
designs therefore required decomposition or rejection.

Every vector is scored from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong) in the fixed order:
novelty (N), primary-source provenance (P), causal validity (V), expected
local impact (I), transfer reliability (R), feasibility/cost (F), numerical
falsifiability (X), and connected-program coherence (C).  A veto overrides a
high sum.  Prestige affects P only; it cannot repair weak scope match or
arithmetic.

Canonicalized keys have the highest standalone score.  The sidecar is placed
first for a dependency reason: current \texttt{\_compact\_doc\_boundaries}
scans the packed CUDA tensor, clones it, calls data-dependent
\texttt{nonzero}, and concatenates the terminal offset inside every charged
training step.  The packer already knows the same literal boundaries.
Transporting immutable, pinned \texttt{int32} offsets with the batch could
remove this synchronization and allocation without changing a single model
input, attention segment, target, parameter, or optimizer update.  If the
measured token-rate gain is large enough, the 300-second frame executes more
updates and tokens.  For token-law slope $b$,
\[
\Delta\bpb_{\mathrm{speed}}\approx-b\log r.
\]
With measured uncertainty $b\in[0.046,0.086]$, central break-even is
$r=\exp(0.002396/0.06)=1.04074$, but robust break-even at the low slope is
\[
                     r_{\rm robust}=1.05347.
\]
Therefore the earlier $r=1.045$ forecast is insufficient under uncertainty:
it predicts only $-0.002025$ at $b=0.046$.  Rank 1 is plausible only because
its semantics can be proven identical and because a strict profile can kill
it before a BPB run.

\section{Candidate-Specific Offline Gates}

\subsection{Round 1: packer-sourced FA3 boundary metadata}

Before scoring, 1000 literal packed batches must match bitwise on inputs,
targets, epochs, \texttt{cu\_seqlens}, maximum sequence length, boundary
counts, and a boundary digest.  Storage must be pinned \texttt{int32};
producer lifetime must extend through a consumer-stream event.  Variable
boundary counts must produce exactly one compiled graph after warmup.
A 20-step equal-step comparison must leave model parameters and optimizer
state bitwise equal.  On a fresh isolated H200, the point token/s ratio must
be at least 1.054 and its 95\% lower bound at least 1.041.  Failure kills the
round without an endpoint score.

\subsection{Round 2: canonicalized Engram keys}

DeepSeek's Engram demo maps token strings through NFKC, NFD, accent stripping,
lowercasing, and whitespace normalization before n-gram hashing
\cite{deepseek2026engram,deepseek2026engramcode}.  The proposed arm changes \emph{memory keys
only}; model input IDs, targets, bytes, document boundaries, and token order
remain fixed.  Special, BOS, and replacement-character IDs remain one-to-one.
At least 10\% of local key cardinality and 10\% of observed n-gram
occurrences must be affected; at least 95\% of alias sets must be explained by
the declared transformations.  Non-memory initialization and global RNG must
match bitwise, charged slowdown must be at most 0.5\%, and compiled graph
count must be unchanged.  The intrinsic prediction is $-0.0030$ BPB; a
0.5\% cost gives an approximate net $-0.002699$.  In the pilot, affected-token
byte-weighted BPB must improve by at least .003 while unaffected-token BPB
regresses by no more than .0015.

\subsection{Round 3: conditioned attention initialization}

The ICLR 2026 source uses independent semi-orthogonal per-head Q/K maps and
the same rectangular-identity V construction for every head
\cite{saratchandran2026conditioned}.  The local fused full-rank identity
instead gives disjoint V coordinate blocks across heads.  It is therefore
named and scored as an extrapolation, not a paper-faithful V replication.
Required preflight conditions are: Q/K row-orthogonality residual at most
$10^{-5}$; V $\sigma_{\min}/\sigma_{\max}\geq0.99$; every non-Q/K/V parameter
and global RNG bitwise equal; initialized attention-Jacobian surrogate down
at least 20\% in every layer; attention entropy change at most .05; finite,
nonzero step-2 Q/K/V gradients; and median step time within .5\%.  The raw
paired prediction is at most $-0.0030$ BPB.  Failure of the mediator kills
the arm before scoring.

\subsection{Round 4: attention-output Peri-RMSNorm}

Peri-LN reports consistent larger-scale loss improvements and attributes
them to normalizing both sides of residual sublayers
\cite{kim2025periln}.  OPHIS already implements the MLP-side form; this round
adds fixed, scale-free RMS normalization only after attention
\texttt{c\_proj}.  The local \texttt{c\_proj} is zero initialized.  Step-0
value parity is thus uninformative: after the first update, RMS normalization
could amplify a tiny branch.  We require step-0 residual output exactly zero,
step-1 \texttt{c\_proj} gradient-norm ratio in $[0.5,2.0]$, no layer above
2.5$\times$ control, and no non-finite value.  Across a 32-step diagnostic,
residual-norm coefficient of variation and max/min layer-gradient ratio must
both fall at least 20\%.  Slowdown must be at most 1\%.  The distinction
between fixed OPHIS RMS normalization and the source's generally learned
scale is part of the claim record, not hidden in implementation.

\subsection{Round 5: pack-preserving compression curriculum}

The EACL 2026 study trained more than 200 models and reports 18--45\% earlier
or mid-training step savings, with compression ratio among the strongest
difficulty signals \cite{zhang2026curriculum}.  The local arm isolates order:
one immutable prepacked cache must give identical whole-epoch pack-ID, token,
target, crop, BOS, byte, and boundary multisets.  Compression score must
separate top and bottom deciles by at least 0.5 pooled SD.  A complete ten-bin
interleaving schedule is frozen and hashed before launch; adaptive checkpoint
selection is forbidden.  Loader/step-time difference must be at most .5\%.
The final prediction is raw $\Delta\bpb\leq-0.0040$; the 25\%-budget probe
must improve by at least .002 and the final endpoint must still clear the
main gate.  An early-only win rejects transfer to the actual objective.

\section{Sequential Funnel, Combination Rule, and GPU Policy}

For each eligible round, stage 1 is one new pair.  Promotion requires raw
$\Delta\bpb\leq-0.002396$, every integrity invariant, and the
candidate-specific mediator.  It remains a screen, not an effect claim.
At $n=3$, require mean $\leq-0.002396$, $3/3$ helping, and a one-sided 95\%
upper bound below zero.  At $n=6$, require mean $\leq-0.002396$, at least
$5/6$ helping, and upper bound $\leq-0.001198$.  At $n=10$, require mean
$\leq-0.002396$, at least $9/10$ helping, and upper bound
$\leq-0.002396$, followed by one never-used test evaluation.  Candidates are
never ranked by their stage-1 means.

An accepted mechanism becomes the comparator for the next round.  A rejected
mechanism leaves the cumulative comparator unchanged.  Thus:
\[
R_0 \rightarrow R_0{+}M_1 \rightarrow
R_0{+}M_1{+}M_2 \rightarrow \cdots ,
\]
with every arrow prospectively attributed.  Interaction tests require a
later paper; no accidental combination is interpreted.

GPU occupancy is subordinate to causal integrity.  Stage 1 uses one pair and
keeps the protocol's one-to-two-GPU reserve.  A promoted $n=3$ stage can use
three concurrent pairs across six tenant-free GPUs.  No unrelated
four-question/all-eight wave is allowed merely to maximize utilization:
doing so would recreate Q1's attribution exposure and violate the sequential
combination rule.  Two clean UUID/PID inventories, immutable assignments,
continuous co-tenancy sampling, and fail-closed per-arm watchdogs are
mandatory.

\section{Reproducibility and Failure Attribution}

For every selected source, the campaign must preserve the official paper or
landing-page identity, retrieval metadata, SHA-256, atomic claims, claim
scope, limitations, evidence assessment, and official code snapshot when
available.  DeepSeek's Engram demo is code provenance for canonicalization,
not proof of a local BPB effect.  Conditioned initialization and Peri-LN have
archived official proceedings PDFs.  Curriculum uses the official ACL
Anthology artifact.  The sidecar is a local systems mechanism and must bind
its equivalence harness and profiler rather than borrow efficacy from
FlashAttention-3 \cite{dao2024fa3}.

Every code change is default-inert, fail-closed, echoed in
\texttt{RESOLVED\_CONFIG}, bound to the intervention record, tested locally,
synced to the governed remote runtime, SHA-matched, and reconciled before a
new challenge-selection event.  Each endpoint closes through RunRecords,
EvidenceRecords, EvidenceUpdates, and Decisions.  Failure reports must name
which link broke: source, config, initialization, mediator, compile graph,
clock, device/PID isolation, completion, or statistical gate.

\section{Explicit Casualties and Limits}

The critic hard-vetoes every Q1 retry or ``qualified 0.927 control'' claim.
RoPE/NoPE, Differential Attention, mHC, FP8, HiMuon, and the current
mass-router formulation fail scope, runtime, arithmetic, duplication, or
dependency checks.  The small-batch source directly supports Adam
second-moment token half-life, including
$0.95^{147456/262144}=0.9715597802$, but does not support simultaneously
transporting that rule to Muon and sparse RMSProp \cite{marek2025smallbatch}.
Its proposed four-arm form is also not executable by the ordinary scheduler.
Physical table packing, sparse kernels, stack layouts, per-row clocks,
curriculum$\times$LR, and Memory+ remain deferred children.  Blockwise
sharpness remains an offline attribution diagnostic.

Ratings are judgments, not outcomes.  Canonicalized keys may collapse too few
of an 8192-token vocabulary.  Sidecar bookkeeping may be too small to clear
robust break-even.  Conditioned initialization may be neutral under
zero-\texttt{c\_proj}; Peri normalization may destabilize that same bootstrap.
Curriculum gains may vanish at the final rather than early endpoint.  These
are not caveats to explain away afterward; they are the preregistered
falsifiers.

\section{Conclusion}

The plateau response is broad but not indiscriminate.  Independent agents
generated ideas from strong 2025--2026 primary work; an adversarial agent
penalized extrapolation, duplication, weak local scope, and impossible
scheduling.  The result is a five-round dependency body with explicit scores,
mechanisms, mediators, kill gates, and a valid concurrent recipe comparator.
The system will first prove that it can observe what changed and attribute
why a round failed.  Only then may a lower BPB become evidence of improvement.
