## Provenance of derived numbers

E5 audits every number in this document against the registry built from
`runs/sweep/results`. One figure here is a DERIVATION, not a measurement, and is shown
as such rather than removed, because the argument it supports is real.

`0.0209` (pragmatist-11): the end-of-run multiplier on the identity-table weight norm at `embwd=0.01`. Not from any run — it is the build-time guard in `tools/mech_lib.py::_embwd`, computed as `(1 - lr_eff * wd) ** steps` with `lr_eff = 0.6 * (512/768) ** -0.5 * 0.75 = 0.551134` (the embedding-group AdamW learning rate, scaled by the mean LR multiplier over the run) and `steps = 700`, giving `(1 - 0.00551135) ** 700 = 0.020887`, which the guard prints rounded as 0.0209. AdamW here is decoupled (`p.mul_(1 - lr_t * wd_t)`), which is what makes the product `lr*wd` the governing quantity rather than `wd` alone. No result record produced this value and none should be cited as if it had.
