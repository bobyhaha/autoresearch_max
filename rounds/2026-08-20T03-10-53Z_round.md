## pragmatist (pragmatist-10)

All eight are pure `train.py` string substitutions; none touches `prepare.py`. Only `prefetch` even reaches into the frozen module's internals — it consumes `make_dataloader`'s generator and its single shared pinned `cpu_buffer` from a second thread — but it does so without editing it, so the contract holds.

**The pricing instrument.** `estimate_flops` ignores elementwise ops, so "zero added FLOPs" in the mech_lib docstrings means zero *estimated* FLOPs, not zero cost. Two calibrations from `runs/sweep/results`, both at the platform cfg: `noqknorm` (removes `q,k = norm(q),norm(k)`, `flops_per_token_M` identical at 239.078) ran 2079 steps (n=4) vs 1989 control (n=33) — **two per-layer norms are worth 4.5% of step count**. And `swdiv` 2→16 cut FLOPs 13.8% (239.078→206.048) for +7.9% steps — a FLOP elasticity of ~0.57, so roughly half the step is GPU-critical. At −0.02921 bpb/e-fold, 4.5% of steps ≈ **0.00135 bpb**, the entire within-wave band.

**Ranked, expected gain per unit of implementation risk:**

1. **prefetch** — biggest upside, only zero-run family. Bound it honestly: `loader_frac` 0.660 (100ms) + `fwdbwd_frac` 0.0586 (8.9ms) of a 151.3ms step is Python; the residual ~42ms is where the main thread actually blocks (`cuda.synchronize`, `.item()`) and releases the GIL. That, not `loader_frac`, is the ceiling: ~+38% steps ≈ 0.0095 bpb, and the GIL means the producer competes for the one scarce resource. Do **not** use `gpu_slack_frac` (0.00038) as evidence either way — the events bracket the whole step and the span includes idle. The data-contract guards are genuinely closed (own stream, event scoped to copy+clone, `record_stream`), and the falsifiers are machine-checkable from fields every record already emits (`final_epoch`, `tokens_per_step`, `grad_accum`). Note its own mechanism id — `loader_slack_prices_gpu_flops_at_zero_merged` — *is* the premise L019 retired; the arm survives on the 42ms blocked window, not on that premise.
2. **vefreeze** — zero FLOPs and one *fewer* optimizer group over 33% of params, so step-positive. Buy it for capacity, not bpb: it is the pathway-vs-content prior for the 8 already-queued `ngram` entries and can retire them. Sequence it ahead of R10NGRAM if the queue can be reordered. Minor gap: only the treatment prints `ve_trainable_params`, so the control has no value to compare (L016).
3. **winsched** — largest structural upside (FLOP-reducing, on the confirmed `swdiv` lever) **only if pre-warmed**. `window_size` is a Python tuple read from `self.window_sizes[i]` inside `torch.compile(model, dynamic=False)`; dynamo specializes on it, so each of the ~4 quantised values is a fresh fwd+bwd graph. Steps ≤10 are the only unbilled window, so three mid-run recompiles are charged in full — `total_seconds − training_seconds` is 182.6s median, mostly initial compile, so tens of seconds each, 20–40% of the budget. `winsched_distinct` counts them but does not price them. Fix inside train.py: cycle `set_short_window` over every quantised value with a dummy fwd/bwd before step 11, and cap K at 2–3 (start = `_WS_FULL/4`). Unfixed, kill it.
4. **ffnpost** — one norm removed, one added: genuinely step-neutral, the only free architecture probe here. Acceptable filler.

**KILLS.** **periln** — two full-width norms per block, i.e. exactly `noqknorm` in reverse: it starts 0.00135 bpb down and must out-earn the whole band to break even, to fix a depth-30+ instability that depth 8 with QK-norm, zero-init `c_proj` and learned `resid/x0_lambdas` does not have. **vnorm** — one added norm (~0.00065 in the hole) *and* `norm(v)` lands after the VE mix, erasing the magnitude channel of `2*sigmoid(ve_gate)`; a result is uninterpretable against ve_placement. **ropefrac** — its own claim reports parity (23.25 vs 22.99), and the split-pair rewrite trades a little arithmetic for slices and three `cat`s that inductor may not fuse; best case is a null. **embwd** — truly free (`p.mul_(1-lr*wd)` already executes; wd is a 0-D tensor, no recompile), but it is a scalar knob wearing a mechanism label, applied to lm_head/wte/value_embeds at once so neither a null nor a harm is attributable, and the one wd reading on disk (`wd_const` 0.4 → 1.001723) is a large harm.

**Budget.** 32 unrun entries are sixteen 2-wide waves; `next_batch` launches one wave per pass, so 4 free GPUs carry two waves at a time — ≥64 min at perfect availability, hours on a shared box. Round 10 can afford **two arms, 16 entries, and no third**. Take `prefetch` and `vefreeze`; hold `winsched` for round 11 once the pre-warm is written.

**Minimum counterbalanced design.** The door (L006) only requires ≥2 distinct slot positions, so 2 waves × [treat, ctrl] / [ctrl, treat] = 4 runs passes. That is not enough to resolve anything: nothing enforces *GPU* counterbalance, GPU claiming is opportunistic, and the per-GPU control means span 0.00174 (gpu4 0.985571 → gpu7 0.983830), 12× the 0.000140 resolution. Use **4 pairs, 8 entries, slots 0/1/0/1** — the R10NGRAM shape — which puts 2×SE of the paired mean at ~0.00014, and verify post hoc that treatment and control covered the same GPU set.

## critic (critic-10)

Lessons I checked these against: L080 (ratio rule), L011 (0.2% of step buys zero steps; break-even ~1.25%), L019 (GPU is not idle, mfu ~42.6%), L041/L044/L080 (the swdiv ladder), L035 (ve density beat its transfer-1 "pathway not content" prior), L048 (joint effect credited to a factor), L045 (step-indexed momentum), L015, L042, L063, L070 (blocks noqknorm), L062/L067/L077 (adoption orphans arms), L005, L029, L052.

**winsched — not runnable as specified; my strongest reject.** Two independent kills. (1) *Arithmetic.* `total_training_time` accrues from step 11 (baseline/train.py:585), so exactly one compile is free and every later recompile is charged. At PLATFORM `swdiv:2` the full short span is 1024; the power-of-two schedule from 128 therefore takes four distinct values and pays **three** charged fwd+bwd recompiles. Budget check: at analyze.py's -0.02921 bpb/e-fold, losing 0.5% of 300s (1.5 seconds *total*) costs +0.000147 — i.e. the entire compile budget before the arm corrupts its own measurement is under two seconds. Meanwhile the ceiling on its throughput credit is small: L041/L044 measured the whole 1024→256 span reduction as 239.078→210.767 MFLOP/token, so a ramp averaging ~10% fewer FLOPs over 64% of the run buys ~3-4% steps ≈ -0.001. Three recompiles cannot fit inside that. (2) *Confound.* Proposed against a PLATFORM control it compares a schedule with a smaller *average* window against a constant 1024 — and swdiv has 180/16/4/4 runs at 2/4/8/16 showing constant 512 (-0.002463), 256 (-0.003360) and 128 (-0.002742) all beat 1024. Any winsched "win" is the average-window effect the ladder already owns, credited to curriculum: precisely L048. The paper is transfer 2 at 1B/100B tokens with an 8K target; our long layers are already at seq 2048 and our short span is the thing four rungs have been shrinking. `winsched_distinct` is insufficient — it counts recompiles without pricing them, and `step_ms_med` is a median, so three catastrophic steps are invisible while fully charged. Require a cumulative compile-time observable and a constant-window control at the schedule's time-average span, or drop it.

**ropefrac — reject, refuted before launch by L011.** Rotary is 3 flops × 1024 channels × 8 layers = 0.0246 of 239.078 MFLOP/token = **0.0103%**. Removing 87.5% of that saves 0.009% of FLOPs — twenty times *smaller* than the 0.2% that L011 proved bought literally zero steps against a ~1.25% break-even. The docstring's "less memory traffic" is false as written: mech_lib.py:447-453 still reads both halves and re-materialises the unrotated tail through two `torch.cat`s, so traffic is unchanged and kernel count rises. Throughput credit is provably zero, so under L080 the ratio is unfavourable at any quality cost — and its own source reports 10% RoPE at 23.25 vs 22.99 full, i.e. ~1% *worse*, transfer 1. Predicted effect: zero-to-harmful. The on-direction, free substitute is the untouched `rope` base literal (make_variant.py:571).

**vefreeze — reject on evidence already bought.** L035 is exactly this experiment's answer: the campaign already ran ve density against three registered "pathway not table content" opposes-claims scored transfer 1-2 and they did not carry at 50M/300s. `embA_engram_image_pathway_not_memory` is another transfer-1, FID-metric member of that same family; buying it a second time is paying twice. Freezing removes 16.78M learnable params for a credit of one AdamW group, and ve params are outside flops_per_token (L015/L019) so there is no step-count gain to trade against. It is also entangled with R9VE1 (ve=1) mid-queue: an adoption would orphan it (L062/L067). Structurally it is a null test with no pre-registered equivalence margin, so it is **not runnable at n=4** — you cannot resolve "no difference" against resolution_at(4)=0.000140 without stating the margin first.

**ngram — keep first, but sharpen.** It is the only arm backed by a transfer-4 claim measured at d_model 512, vocab 8192, Muon+Adam, val BPB. 32768×512 = 16.78M zero-FLOP params — numerically identical to the ve=1 addition L035 measured at ~2% step cost and a win. Two caveats to pre-register: it is a *bigram*, one table, constant gate, against a paper using five orders with per-order tables plus a learned gate, so do not import -0.042; and wte already supplies the unigram pathway, so this measures only the marginal bigram. Check the epoch gate before val_bpb (L042), because L035's gpu4 cell voided at final_epoch 1.0 on exactly this parameter-adding profile.

**Agenda.** Yes, this is dodging. `signal_scale` has zero runs, three untouched axes (rope/softcap/x0init), is priced free, and is the only family on the table with zero step-count and zero L045 momentum-ramp confound. Two of the operator's top three are attention arms in a family with 24 runs. `vnorm` (entangled with ve_placement while R9VE1 is in flight) and `ffnpost` (rescales the stream resid_lambdas/x0_lambdas multiply) should not run until R9VE1 lands. `prefetch` still leans on the L019-retired premise.

## synthesis (synth-10)
