## What this round corrected

**The tightness claim was checked on a favourable subset.** L061 reported the new
instrument at 0.42x the old sd from a pooled within-GPU sd of 0.000079 -- computed
over the eight RECAL18 runs alone, launched back-to-back in one quiet window. Over
all twelve exact-cfg controls it is 0.000104 against 0.000196, a ratio of 0.53x.
The falsifier still does not fire; the reported number was too flattering. L065.

**The critic's alternative explanation is refuted, but its warning stands.** It
argued a quiet half-hour on gpu6/gpu7 could masquerade as a better operating point.
At tbs=19 those two devices were the NOISIEST of the four (0.000210 and 0.000249
against gpu4's 0.000176 and gpu5's 0.000132) and are now the quietest, so the
devices that tightened are the ones that were worst. What survives is thinness:
two devices, five runs each, and gpu4/gpu5 carry one control apiece.

**Adoption had made the retired operating point unanalysable.** step_law fitted only
on is_platform runs, so the tbs=19 law silently returned None the moment tbs=18 was
adopted -- which is why L063's step-law adjustment could not be reproduced an hour
after it was written. It now takes an explicit baseline. The retired law refits on
93 controls and L063 reproduces: credit -0.002191, quality cost +0.010660 against a
raw +0.008469.

**And that fix was only half-applied.** synth-8 found that step_law_explains -- the
caller that actually computes the per-pair share -- never forwarded the baseline, so
it kept returning None for exactly the historical pairs the parameter was added to
rescue. Fixing a function and not its only caller is the build-and-not-connect
failure this campaign keeps paying for. Fixed; it now reproduces the figures above.

**The activation pre-check was half a check, and it had shipped twice.** It verified
only that CONTROLS FAIL a rule, never that a treatment could satisfy one. L050
diagnosed that shape four hours earlier and recorded the fix as not yet built; the
identical defect then shipped as qk_q_rms_final lt 1.0. Now built (L064).
