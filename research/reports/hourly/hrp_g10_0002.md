## Abstract

After repairing the no-op intervention defect reported last period, eight levers were tested with genuinely working implementations (AttnRes naive and refined, token-shift, C1, C3, C1xC3, sparse-grad, rowwise state, nGPT hypersphere). Every one regressed, and every one shared a single mechanical signature: it lost 80 to 150 optimizer steps inside the fixed 300-second budget. In a wall-clock frame that step loss is paid directly in val_bpb, so the mechanisms were not the binding constraint. Two corrections came out of that sweep: NGRAM_SPARSE_GRAD, recorded in this campaign's own notes as a 9.3 percent step-time win, costs 148 steps on the reference box, the deciding variable being the torch version (2.13 on the dev box where the win was measured, 2.9.1 here), so the lever is version-gated rather than universal; and NGRAM_STATE_ROWWISE is exactly free (delta bpb +0.00004, delta steps -1) while cutting optimizer state, so it is banked as headroom rather than claimed as a win. The block's central result then inverted the picture. Intra-document attention masking, implemented densely, is catastrophic (1.012956 at 522 steps); implemented block-sparse via FlexAttention at identical masking semantics it reaches 0.943750 at 1084 steps, a gain of -0.0692 val_bpb and 2.08x the throughput from an implementation change alone. Against a matched-backend control it is a confirmed -0.0111 over two paired seeds, 2.7x the 2-sigma gate, while simultaneously gaining 101 steps. A block-size sweep proved monotonic (64 better than 128 better than 256; 32 fails on a FlexAttention minimum-block constraint), and at BLOCK=64 the flex path recovers essentially the whole step gap to fa3. Six paired seeds against the fa3 baseline give a mean of -0.001415 with 6/6 the same sign and t=-3.45 (p about 0.018): statistically detectable, but well inside the preregistered 2-sigma adoption gate of 0.004072, so it is NOT declared SOTA. Finally, the hypothesis that doc-masking would rescue the memorizer-regularization family was tested and falsified: C1 costs +0.0222 with doc-masking and +0.0222 without it, so its harm is not about cross-document contamination.

## Methods and campaign accounting

All arms run on the reference H200 at STOP_MODE=time, TIME_BUDGET=300, COMPILE_MODE=max-autotune-no-cudagraphs, frozen 10-shard split, seed-matched. Doc-mask arms necessarily run ATTN_BACKEND=sdpa because the mask path is gated on it, so a MODE=window control isolates the document-restriction effect from the mandatory fa3-to-sdpa swap, and every doc-mask claim is stated against a matched-backend control rather than across backends. Kernel refinement of AttnRes was verified by VRAM and step-time as well as val_bpb. The block-size knob DOC_MASK_BLOCK was added (default 128 equals the FlexAttention default, so unset is unchanged). Paired deltas were computed per seed and summarized with a paired t statistic; the adoption decision uses the preregistered 2-sigma gate, not the p-value.

## Findings

- **preliminary_observation** — At identical masking semantics, replacing the dense (B,1,T,T) document mask with a block-sparse FlexAttention BlockMask changes val_bpb from 1.012956 at 522 steps to 0.943750 at 1084 steps: -0.0692 val_bpb and 2.08x the throughput from the implementation alone.
  - Limitation: Single seed for the dense arms; the effect size is far larger than any plausible noise, but the dense arms were not replicated because their cost made them clearly non-competitive.
- **preliminary_observation** — Against a matched sdpa control, flex doc-masking is a confirmed win of -0.0111 over two paired seeds (-0.00923 and -0.01291), 2.7x the 2-sigma gate, while also gaining 101 steps.
  - Limitation: Two paired seeds; both same sign and far past the gate, but this is the matched-backend comparison, not a comparison against the campaign's fa3 baseline.
- **preliminary_observation** — Against the fa3 baseline, flex doc-masking at BLOCK=64 gives a mean paired delta of -0.001415 over six seeds with 6/6 the same sign and t=-3.45, which is statistically detectable but does NOT clear the preregistered 2-sigma adoption gate of 0.004072.
  - Limitation: Deliberately NOT promoted: a refinement smaller than the measured noise floor is not an adoptable result under this campaign's own rule, regardless of its p-value. Recorded as a promising sub-gate candidate.
- **preliminary_observation** — Adding doc-masking does not change C1's verdict: n-gram-table weight decay costs +0.0222 with doc-masking and +0.0222 without it.
  - Limitation: Single seed per arm; the two independent measurements agreeing to four decimal places is what makes the falsification credible, not the individual precision.

## Negative and null results

- Eight levers tested with working implementations all regressed, each losing 80-150 steps: AttnRes (naive and refined), token-shift, C1, C3, C1xC3, sparse-grad, and the nGPT hypersphere residual.
- The nGPT hypersphere residual was the cheapest lever tested (-36 steps) yet collapsed quality; its alpha sweep is non-monotonic with an interior optimum at 0.25, indicating a real mechanism whose residual-only implementation is a half-system.
- NGRAM_SPARSE_GRAD is harmful on the reference box (-148 steps), contradicting a stored note that recorded it as a 9.3 percent win on a different torch version.
- DOC_MASK_BLOCK=32 fails with an InductorError from a FlexAttention minimum-block constraint, closing the block-size direction at 64.
- The hypothesis that doc-masking would rescue the memorizer-regularization family is falsified.

## Limitations

- All doc-mask arms run on sdpa because the mask path is gated on that backend; the doc-mask benefit (-0.011) nearly cancels the sdpa penalty (+0.012), which is why the fa3 comparison lands near parity.
- Getting doc-masking to fa3 speed is blocked by a concrete obstacle: this build exposes flash_attn_varlen_func only as an autograd.Function, not as the low-level custom-op pair, and letting torch.compile with fullgraph trace into an autograd.Function is the documented cause of this box's historical segfault.
- Several arms in the final rounds ran six-wide on the shared box; the observed within-round spread on a repeated configuration was about 0.0012, which bounds how finely these single-seed comparisons can be read.

## Portfolio decisions

- Do NOT declare SOTA for flex doc-masking at BLOCK=64 despite 6/6 same-sign paired improvements: the effect is sub-gate.
- Adopt BLOCK=64 as the flex doc-mask setting for any future doc-mask work, since the block-size sweep is monotonic and 32 is infeasible.
- Retire the hypothesis that cross-document contamination explains C1's harm.
- Correct the stored NGRAM_SPARSE_GRAD note to version-gated rather than universal.
- Treat any future wall-clock regression as evidence about an implementation until its kernel is competitive, per the AttnRes and doc-mask trajectories.
