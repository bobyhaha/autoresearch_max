## Abstract

The block-15 multi-agent planning round's adversarial critic reported it could not find ATTNRES_ENABLE, NGRAM_WD_LAMBDA, or NGRAM_BACKOFF_KAPPA anywhere in the local checkout despite block-14 having reported concrete numbers for them. Direct verification confirmed the critic was right, and the defect had TWO independent causes, either alone sufficient to silently turn a treatment arm into its own control. (1) The three mechanisms never existed in the canonical train.py -- zero case-insensitive matches across 3358 lines, all five sota_snapshots, and the entire git history of the file; they lived only in forked one-off scripts on the remote box (repo/train_attnres_c1_v2.py, repo/train_c3.py). (2) The intervention records declared only descriptive lowercase parameters, so tools/run_gated.py:_arm_env resolved env={} and exported no flag at all. The gated runner still reported config_verified=True, because verify_config iterates the bound environment and an empty environment verifies trivially. The three block-14 deltas (-0.000839, -0.001281, -0.001019) are therefore not null results about AttnRes, C1, or C3; they are the same configuration measured twice, which is exactly why all three landed an order of magnitude inside the 2-sigma gate. They are retracted as invalid. The mechanisms have now been ported into the canonical train.py verbatim from the reference forks (each an exact no-op when off), a latent autograd bug in the reference AttnRes was found and fixed (preallocated stack buffers mutated in place invalidate the views backward requires; replaced with list+stack), all three flags are echoed in RESOLVED_CONFIG so config-verify can observe them, properly env-bound intervention records were added under new ids so frozen fingerprints stay valid, and two fail-closed guards were added to run_gated. A four-arm full-budget verification then produced large, unambiguous signal for the first time.

## Findings

- **contradicted** — ATTNRES_ENABLE, NGRAM_WD_LAMBDA and NGRAM_BACKOFF_KAPPA were absent from the canonical train.py and the intervention records bound no environment, so every treatment arm launched against train.py ran a configuration byte-identical to its control while reporting config_verified=True. (evidence: `evd_run_block14r2_attnres_noop`, `evd_run_block14r2_c1_noop`, `evd_run_block14r2_c1c3_noop`)
  - Limitation: Established by code audit rather than controlled experiment; no experiment can distinguish a no-op treatment from its control by construction, which is why it went undetected.
- **preliminary_observation** — With working implementations, all three mechanisms regress substantially at the current 10-shard operating point: versus a flags-off arm at val_bpb 0.941657, AttnRes gives +0.0146, C1 at lambda=0.02 gives +0.0222, and the C1+C3 combination gives +0.0236 -- each 3.5x to 5.8x the frame's 2-sigma gate of 0.004072.
  - Limitation: Single seed, executed as direct verification runs rather than through the gated launcher, so these are diagnostic measurements establishing that the mechanisms now function -- not gated verdicts. They must be re-run through tools/run_stage.py with paired seeds before any adopt/reject decision.
  - Limitation: The flags-off arm reproduced the reconciled 10-seed baseline (0.939797, sigma 0.002036) to within one sigma, supporting the claim that OFF is a genuine no-op.
- **preliminary_observation** — The reference AttnRes implementation carried a latent autograd defect: preallocated (L+1,B,T,d) stack buffers written in place invalidate the sliced views backward requires, raising a version-counter RuntimeError.
  - Limitation: Observed directly in the first GPU smoke test after porting and fixed by appending to a list and stacking at use time, then re-verified crash-free at full budget. This matches, and plausibly explains, the AttnRes crash already recorded in paper 013's negative results, but that correspondence is inferred rather than separately re-measured.

## Negative and null results

- Three block-14 experiments are retracted as invalid rather than reported as nulls: exp_block14_attnres_r2, exp_block14_c1_10sh_r2, exp_block14_c1c3_r2.
- Once genuinely executed, none of AttnRes, C1, or the C1+C3 combination improves val_bpb at this operating point; all three regress well beyond the noise floor.
- The prior CUDA-graph capture rejection is unaffected by this defect: that arm failed by reproducible segfault under two different batch configurations, an execution failure rather than a silent no-op.

## Limitations

- The four-arm verification is single-seed and was not launched through the gated runner, so it establishes mechanism functionality and effect direction, not an adoption verdict.
- Whether the mechanisms would help at a different operating point (for example 5 shards, where the memorizer-regularization family predicts its benefit concentrates) is untested here; the active frame's scope_key is frozen to the 10-shard split.

## Portfolio decisions

- Retract the three affected block-14 results as invalid; they are not evidence about the named mechanisms.
- Port all three mechanisms into the canonical train.py and echo them in RESOLVED_CONFIG so config-verify covers treatment flags, not only frozen run controls.
- Register properly env-bound intervention records under NEW ids rather than bumping existing versions, so every frozen experiment fingerprint referencing the originals stays valid and history is not rewritten.
- Make run_gated fail closed when a treatment intervention resolves to an empty environment, and report a bound key absent from RESOLVED_CONFIG distinctly from a value mismatch.
- Re-run the affected hypotheses through the gated launcher against the repaired harness before any adopt or reject verdict is recorded.
